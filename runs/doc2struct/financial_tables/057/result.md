## Vesting of 2022 PSP LTI awards

**Relative Total Shareholder Return 30%** + **Total Sales Growth 20%** + **Core Operating Profit Growth 20%** + **Pipeline Progress 20%** + **RB: Environment 10%** = **Total Vested LTI**

### **Overview of PSP LTI performance**

In line with the Committee’s agreed principles, actual performance against each measure is carefully reviewed and adjustments are made, as appropriate. This ensures that the vesting outcome reflects genuine underlying business performance and has been delivered in line with our culture and values. The Committee did not deem it necessary to exercise any discretion in relation to the vesting of the awards or due to share price changes. Overall, 80.75% of the 2022 PSP awards vested against the targets set out below.
